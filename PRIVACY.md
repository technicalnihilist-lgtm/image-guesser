# Privacy Policy — Image Guesser

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

**Short version:** Image Guesser has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Nothing about
you leaves your device except the plain image requests needed to show a photo.

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following in your device's local storage (Android WebView storage / browser
`localStorage`):

- your coin balance, best streak and answered count,
- the list of items you have already seen (so questions do not repeat),
- a small cache of image credits and recently fetched items,
- your settings: sound, music, vibration, theme, blur, language.

This data never leaves the device. It is not transmitted, synced or backed up to any server we
control. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or using **Settings → Reset game progress** removes it.

## Network requests

To display a real photograph, the app requests data from:

- **en.wikipedia.org** — the MediaWiki API, for item names, descriptions, random items and image credits,
- **upload.wikimedia.org / thumb.wikimedia.org** — Wikimedia's image servers, for the photographs.

Those requests contain no personal information beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP
address, which Wikimedia needs to serve the file, and your browser's user agent). Wikimedia's own
privacy policy governs them: <https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy>.

No request is made to any advertising, analytics or tracking service. The app contains no SDKs of
that kind.

## Permissions (Android)

- **INTERNET** — to fetch photos and credits.
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** — to show the "you are offline" notice.
- **VIBRATE** — for haptic feedback, only if you leave Vibration switched on.

## Children

Image Guesser is a general-audience educational quiz. It collects no personal information from
anyone, including children, and shows no advertising.
